## Scope: T8.2 only — the resolution-free diagnostics view

T8.1 and T8.3 are done and on your branch: `pnpm freeze` writes a SHA-256-hashed JSONL manifest,
`pnpm resolve` appends outcomes without ever mutating a forecast. You are building the view that
renders what can be measured **before any market resolves**.

Read `docs/05-ai/EVALUATION.md`, especially the resolution-free diagnostics section and §B8.

## Why "resolution-free" is the whole design

ADR-0007 refuses a backtest: a model trained on the internet has likely seen the outcomes of
already-resolved markets, so retrospective accuracy would be contaminated. That leaves a real
problem — with no resolved outcomes yet, **there is no accuracy number to show at all**.

The answer is diagnostics that are meaningful without outcomes:

- **Complementary coherence.** For a two-outcome market, do the model's Yes and No estimates sum
  to 1? Incoherence is a defect visible with zero resolutions.
- **Sample dispersion.** The IQR across k samples, distribution across the frozen set.
- **Blind-vs-anchored delta.** How far the estimate moves when the price is shown. A delta near
  zero is evidence the blind elicitation was not blind.
- **The disagreement distribution.** How far model and market diverge across the set.
- **The gate reason histogram.** Which rules actually fire, and how often.

None of these say the model is *right*. They say whether it is *self-consistent* and whether the
elicitation worked. Do not let the copy blur that distinction.

## The honesty constraints are the acceptance criteria here

- **No metric without its bin or sample count, visibly.** A number computed from three entries
  says so next to the number, not in a tooltip.
- **No metric without its methodology one click away.** How it was computed, in plain language.
- **The manifest hash is displayed and must match the file.** That is the tamper-evidence claim
  made checkable; assert the match in a test.
- **With no resolved outcomes, the honest statement is that predictions are recorded and not yet
  scored.** Never imply accuracy has been demonstrated. Never state or imply the system beats the
  market. `EVALUATION.md` §B8 binds every word.
- An empty manifest renders as an explicit empty state saying so, not a blank panel or zeros.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every diagnostic renders with its bin or sample counts visible.
2. No metric is displayed without its methodology one click away.
3. The manifest hash is displayed in the UI and a test asserts it matches the file.
4. An empty or tiny manifest renders honestly, with the sample size stated.
5. No copy implies accuracy has been measured, or that the system beats the market.
