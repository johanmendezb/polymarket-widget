## Scope: T5.4 only — the AI panel UI

Everything upstream exists: the blind prompt (T5.1), the client and k-sampling (T5.2), the blend,
gate and `POST /api/ai/forecast` (T5.3), and the widget states (T4.2–T4.5). You are building the
panel that renders a `Recommendation`, and nothing else.

Read `docs/01-product/USER_FLOWS.md` for the panel's states and `docs/05-ai/EVALUATION.md` §B8
for the claims policy, which is binding on every word of copy you write.

## The three registers are the product

The entire thesis is that a prediction market is three different numbers most interfaces blend
into one:

- **what the market believes** — the price
- **what the model estimates** — the forecast
- **what it would cost you to act** — the fill plus fees

They must render in **visibly different registers**. Not three numbers in the same typeface a
few pixels apart. A user should never have to work out which one they are looking at. This is an
acceptance criterion, not styling preference.

The model's estimate is **never blended into the price** for display. The blend exists in the
recommendation; the raw market price stays visible and distinct.

## Provenance is not optional

Every displayed forecast shows **timestamp, model id, prompt version, sample count `k`, and
dispersion**. A forecast without its provenance is a claim rather than a measurement, and the
Definition of Done rejects on honesty before it rejects on polish.

Dispersion is the uncertainty measure. There is no self-reported confidence and you must not
invent one, or render dispersion as though it were confidence.

## Abstention renders as a result, not a failure

`insufficient_evidence` and a fired gate are **success paths**. "I could not find sources I
trust for this question" is a correct answer and the product presents it as one — not in error
styling, not as a retry prompt, not as an empty state that looks broken.

When the gate fires, **name its reason codes** in the UI. A recommendation the user cannot
interrogate is worse than no recommendation.

## The claims policy binds your copy

- Never state or imply the system beats the market, is more accurate than the market, or should
  be trusted over it.
- Evidence renders with its sources and dates. **An undated source is labelled undated** rather
  than presented as current.
- No fabricated source, statistic or backtest anywhere. If a field is empty, say it is empty.

## Resilience

Killing the AI route must leave search, market detail, the cost preview and the simulated bet
fully working. E6 asserts this with an E2E test, so do not introduce a coupling that would break
it — the AI panel is an independent data path that can fail on its own.

Handle every AI error state distinctly: `AI_TIMEOUT`, `AI_INVALID_OUTPUT`, `AI_NO_EVIDENCE`.
Each gets copy that says what actually happened.

No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. Model output is text, and it is untrusted text at that.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The AI estimate and the market price render in visibly different registers.
2. Every displayed forecast shows timestamp, model id, prompt version, k and dispersion.
3. The gate firing renders as a result with its reason codes named, not as an error.
4. `AI_NO_EVIDENCE` renders as an explicit "no usable evidence" state, not error styling.
5. With the AI route returning 500, the rest of the widget still completes the golden path.
6. No copy anywhere states or implies the system beats the market.
